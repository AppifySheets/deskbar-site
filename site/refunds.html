Refunds

Last updated 25 October 2026

Deskbar Pro is not on sale yet. This is the policy that will apply once it is.

Deskbar Pro is sold through the Microsoft Store: US$18.99, paid once, in the United States, and a local price set by Microsoft elsewhere. The Store offers a free 15-day trial with every Pro feature on, so please use it to check that Deskbar works on your setup before you buy.

Microsoft sells Deskbar Pro as the reseller, so Store purchases follow Microsoft's refund policy. To ask for a refund, sign in at account.microsoft.com, open Order history and choose Request a refund. Microsoft decides whether to refund; we cannot refund a Store purchase ourselves. If you think you were charged twice, ask Microsoft the same way.
