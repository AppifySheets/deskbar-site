Privacy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Deskbar, the app, collects nothing about you and sends nothing about you anywhere. Your workspaces, window names, rules and time per workspace stay in files under %APPDATA%\Deskbar on your own computer. Its only network traffic is the update check: every five minutes, and whenever you choose Check for updates, it asks GitHub whether a newer release exists, and accepting an update downloads it from GitHub. Those requests reach GitHub like any other web request, with your IP address, and GitHub's own privacy statement covers them. You can turn the automatic check off in Manage. This site sets no cookies and runs no scripts or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security, under the same GitHub privacy statement. If you write to hello@deskbar.app, your message and address are used to reply to you and for nothing else.

When Deskbar Pro goes on sale

Paddle will sell Deskbar Pro as the reseller and will handle your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, your email address and the country of purchase, and use them to send your licence key and to answer you about that purchase. The key itself contains your email address, and the app checks it on your computer without contacting anyone.
