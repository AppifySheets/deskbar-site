Privacy

Last updated 25 October 2026

Deskbar, the app, collects nothing about you and sends nothing about you anywhere. This is true of both copies: the free one from GitHub and the one from the Microsoft Store. Your workspaces, window names, rules and time per workspace stay in files under %APPDATA%\Deskbar on your own computer. To show your windows on the bar, the app reads the titles of the windows you have open. That happens on your computer only, and none of it is sent anywhere. The only network requests either copy makes are the ones described below.

If Deskbar ever starts collecting diagnostic or usage data, this page will say what is collected and how to turn it off before the release that starts it.

The free copy from GitHub

Its only network traffic is the update check: every five minutes, and whenever you choose Check for updates, it asks GitHub whether a newer release exists, and accepting an update downloads it from GitHub. Those requests reach GitHub like any other web request, with your IP address, and GitHub's own privacy statement covers them. You can turn the automatic check off in Manage.

The Microsoft Store copy

It never contacts GitHub. It asks the Microsoft Store for its own licence, to know whether it is Deskbar Pro or in its trial: once when it starts, and every five minutes while the automatic check is on. On the same five-minute timer, and whenever you choose Check for updates, it asks the Store whether an update exists, and the Store installs it. Those requests go to Microsoft, under Microsoft's privacy statement. You can turn the automatic check off in Manage.

Buying Deskbar Pro

Deskbar Pro is not on sale yet. It will be sold through the Microsoft Store, with Microsoft as the reseller and merchant of record, so your payment details go to Microsoft under Microsoft's privacy statement. There is no licence key: the Store's licence on your Microsoft account is what makes the Store copy Pro. Microsoft does not send us your name or email address for an ordinary purchase. We see only total sales and payouts.

This site

This site sets no cookies and runs no scripts or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement. If you write to hello@deskbar.app, your message and address are used to reply to you and for nothing else.
